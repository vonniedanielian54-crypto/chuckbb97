Your KD LLC account settings give you full control over how your profile appears, how your venue operates, and how external systems connect to your data. Whether you're a guest managing personal preferences or an operator fine-tuning reservation rules, all configuration lives under **Settings** in your dashboard sidebar.

## Profile Settings

Your profile settings control how KD LLC identifies and communicates with you across the platform. To update them, navigate to **Settings → Profile**.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Display Name** | The name shown to venues and guests during reservations |
| **Email Address** | Used for login, notifications, and account recovery |
| **Phone Number** | Required for SMS notifications and two-factor authentication |
| **Timezone** | Ensures reservation times display correctly for your location |
| **Locale** | Sets your preferred language and date/number formatting |

<Note>
Changing your email address triggers a verification message to the new address. Your old email remains active until you confirm the change.
</Note>

After editing any field, click **Save Changes** to apply your updates immediately.

## Venue Settings

If you operate a venue on KD LLC, the **Venue Settings** panel lets you define the rules that govern how guests discover and book your space. Navigate to **Settings → Venue** to access these controls.

### Booking Rules

- **Default Capacity** — Set the maximum number of guests your venue accepts across all reservations at a given time.
- **Operating Hours** — Define your open and close times for each day of the week. Guests cannot book outside these windows.
- **Booking Lead Time** — The minimum number of hours in advance a guest must create a reservation. For example, a value of `2` prevents same-day bookings less than 2 hours out.
- **Cancellation Policy Window** — The number of hours before a reservation during which a guest may cancel without penalty. After this window closes, your configured late-cancellation policy applies.
- **Auto-Confirm Reservations** — When enabled, incoming reservations are confirmed instantly without requiring manual approval from your team.

### Venue Config Example

The following JSON represents a typical venue configuration as stored and returned by the KD LLC API:

```json
{
  "booking_lead_time_hours": 2,
  "cancellation_window_hours": 24,
  "auto_confirm": true,
  "default_party_size_max": 12,
  "booking_notes_prompt": "Any dietary restrictions or special requests?"
}
```

You can read and write this configuration via the [Venues API](/api/venues). The `booking_notes_prompt` string is displayed to guests during checkout, so write it as a direct question or instruction.

<Tip>
Set `auto_confirm` to `false` if your venue has limited seating or frequently hosts large private events that require manual review before accepting walk-in reservations.
</Tip>

## API Keys

API keys allow external applications, scripts, and integrations to authenticate with the KD LLC API on your behalf. Manage them under **Settings → API Keys**.

### Generating a Key

1. Click **New API Key**.
2. Enter a descriptive label (for example, `pos-integration` or `zapier-automation`).
3. Select the permission scopes the key requires.
4. Click **Create Key**.
5. Copy the key immediately — KD LLC displays it only once.

### Rotating a Key

Rotate a key when you suspect it has been exposed or as part of a regular security audit:

1. Locate the key you want to rotate in the key list.
2. Click **Rotate** next to that key.
3. Confirm the action in the dialog.
4. Update all systems using the old key with the newly generated value before the rotation grace period expires.

<Warning>
The old key remains valid for 24 hours after rotation to give you time to update dependent systems. After that window, the old key is permanently invalidated.
</Warning>

### Revoking a Key

To permanently invalidate a key with no grace period, click **Revoke** next to the key and confirm. Use this immediately if a key is compromised.

## Danger Zone

The **Danger Zone** section at the bottom of **Settings → Account** contains irreversible actions. Treat these with care.

### Deleting Your Account

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, reservation history, saved venues, and all associated data from KD LLC. This action cannot be undone.

To delete your account:

1. Scroll to **Settings → Account → Danger Zone**.
2. Click **Delete Account**.
3. Type your email address to confirm your intent.
4. Click **Permanently Delete**.

<Warning>
If you operate a venue, you must transfer or close all active reservations and remove your venue listing before you can delete your account. Accounts with pending payouts are ineligible for deletion until all balances are settled.
</Warning>
