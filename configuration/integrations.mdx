## Calendar Integrations

Connecting a calendar integration ensures that every confirmed reservation and published event automatically appears in your preferred calendar app. This keeps your schedule accurate without manual entry.

### Supported Calendar Platforms

| Platform | Sync Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Calendar** | KD LLC → Google | OAuth 2.0 authorization; reservations appear as calendar events |
| **Apple Calendar** | KD LLC → Apple | Uses iCal feed subscription; refreshes every 15 minutes |
| **Microsoft Outlook** | KD LLC → Outlook | OAuth 2.0 via Microsoft 365; supports shared calendars |

### Setting Up Google Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in your KD LLC dashboard and locate the **Google Calendar** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Google account">
    Click **Connect**. You are redirected to Google's authorization page. Sign in with the Google account that owns the calendar you want to sync to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Grant KD LLC permission to create and manage calendar events on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select target calendar">
    After authorization, you are returned to KD LLC. Choose the target calendar from the dropdown (for example, a dedicated **KD LLC Reservations** calendar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate sync">
    Click **Save** to activate the sync. New confirmed reservations will appear in your Google Calendar within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cancellations and modifications are reflected in your calendar automatically.

<Note>
To disconnect Google Calendar at any time, return to **Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar** and click **Disconnect**. Existing calendar events created by KD LLC are not deleted from Google Calendar when you disconnect.
</Note>

## POS Integrations

Linking your point-of-sale system to KD LLC lets your front-of-house staff check in guests directly from the POS terminal and automatically associates reservation data with the resulting transaction record.

### Supported POS Systems

- **Toast**
- **Square**
- **Lightspeed Restaurant**
- **Clover**
- **Revel Systems**

To connect a POS system, navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Point of Sale**, select your provider, and follow the provider-specific authorization steps. Most integrations require an API key or OAuth flow initiated from your POS account's developer or integrations settings panel.

<Warning>
POS integrations are available on the **Operator Pro** and **Enterprise** plans only. If you do not see the POS section under Integrations, upgrade your plan under **Settings → Billing**.
</Warning>

## CRM Integrations

CRM integrations push guest profile data and reservation history into your customer relationship management platform, enabling personalized follow-up campaigns, loyalty tracking, and VIP identification.

### Salesforce

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Salesforce integration">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Salesforce** and click **Connect to Salesforce**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Sign in to Salesforce and grant KD LLC the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map fields">
    Use the field-mapping table to align KD LLC guest fields with Salesforce Contact fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sync scope">
    Select whether to sync all guests or only guests with at least one confirmed reservation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable sync">
    Click **Enable Sync** to activate the integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### HubSpot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the HubSpot integration">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → HubSpot** and click **Connect to HubSpot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the integration">
    Follow the OAuth prompt to grant KD LLC access to your HubSpot account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sync triggers">
    Choose which KD LLC events — reservation confirmed, cancellation, repeat visit — create or update HubSpot Contact records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Deal Creation (optional)">
    Toggle **Deal Creation** on to log high-value reservations as HubSpot Deals automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to activate the integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use HubSpot's workflow automation to send a win-back email to guests who have not returned to your venue in 60 days, triggered by the last reservation date synced from KD LLC.
</Tip>

## Zapier and Make (Integromat)

Zapier and Make let you connect KD LLC to thousands of additional apps using pre-built automation templates — no code required.

### Zapier

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the KD LLC app">
    Visit [zapier.com](https://zapier.com) and search for **KD LLC** in the app directory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Browse available Zap templates (for example, *New KD LLC Reservation → Add Row in Google Sheets*) and click **Use this Zap**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate and configure">
    Sign in to your KD LLC account using your API key when prompted, then follow the setup wizard to complete your Zap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Make (Integromat)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new Scenario">
    Open your Make workspace and create a new Scenario.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the KD LLC module">
    Search for and add the **KD LLC** module from the app library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Enter your API key when prompted to authenticate the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your trigger and actions">
    Choose a trigger (for example, **Watch Reservations**) and chain it to any downstream modules your workflow requires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If none of the listed integrations meet your needs, use KD LLC's [webhook notifications](/configuration/notifications#webhook-notifications) to build a fully custom integration. Any service that can receive an HTTP POST request can be connected this way without relying on a third-party automation platform.
</Tip>
