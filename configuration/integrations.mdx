## Calendar Integrations

Connecting a calendar integration ensures that every confirmed reservation and published event automatically appears in your preferred calendar app. This keeps your schedule accurate without manual entry.

### Supported Calendar Platforms

| Platform | Sync Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Calendar** | KD LLC → Google | OAuth 2.0 authorization; reservations appear as calendar events |
| **Apple Calendar** | KD LLC → Apple | Uses iCal feed subscription; refreshes every 15 minutes |
| **Microsoft Outlook** | KD LLC → Outlook | OAuth 2.0 via Microsoft 365; supports shared calendars |

### Setting Up Google Calendar (Step-by-Step)

1. Open **Settings → Integrations** in your KD LLC dashboard.
2. Locate the **Google Calendar** card and click **Connect**.
3. You are redirected to Google's authorization page. Sign in with the Google account that owns the calendar you want to sync to.
4. Grant KD LLC permission to create and manage calendar events on your behalf.
5. After authorization, you are returned to KD LLC. Select the target calendar from the dropdown (for example, a dedicated **KD LLC Reservations** calendar).
6. Click **Save** to activate the sync.

New confirmed reservations will appear in your Google Calendar within seconds. Cancellations and modifications are reflected automatically.

<Note>
To disconnect Google Calendar at any time, return to **Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar** and click **Disconnect**. Existing calendar events created by KD LLC are not deleted from Google Calendar when you disconnect.
</Note>

## POS Integrations

Linking your point-of-sale system to KD LLC lets your front-of-house staff check in guests directly from the POS terminal and automatically associates reservation data with the resulting transaction record.

### Supported POS Systems

- **Toast**
- **Square**
- **Lightspeed Restaurant**
- **Clover**
- **Revel Systems**

To connect a POS system, navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Point of Sale**, select your provider, and follow the provider-specific authorization steps. Most integrations require an API key or OAuth flow initiated from your POS account's developer or integrations settings panel.

<Warning>
POS integrations are available on the **Operator Pro** and **Enterprise** plans only. If you do not see the POS section under Integrations, upgrade your plan under **Settings → Billing**.
</Warning>

## CRM Integrations

CRM integrations push guest profile data and reservation history into your customer relationship management platform, enabling personalized follow-up campaigns, loyalty tracking, and VIP identification.

### Salesforce

1. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Salesforce**.
2. Click **Connect to Salesforce** and authorize KD LLC via OAuth.
3. Map KD LLC guest fields to Salesforce Contact fields using the field-mapping table.
4. Choose whether to sync all guests or only guests with at least one confirmed reservation.
5. Click **Enable Sync**.

### HubSpot

1. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → HubSpot**.
2. Click **Connect to HubSpot** and authorize the integration.
3. Configure which KD LLC events (reservation confirmed, cancellation, repeat visit) create or update HubSpot Contact records.
4. Optionally enable **Deal Creation** to log high-value reservations as HubSpot Deals.
5. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
Use HubSpot's workflow automation to send a win-back email to guests who have not returned to your venue in 60 days, triggered by the last reservation date synced from KD LLC.
</Tip>

## Zapier and Make (Integromat)

Zapier and Make let you connect KD LLC to thousands of additional apps using pre-built automation templates — no code required.

### Zapier

1. Visit [zapier.com](https://zapier.com) and search for **KD LLC** in the app directory.
2. Browse available Zap templates (for example, *New KD LLC Reservation → Add Row in Google Sheets*).
3. Click **Use this Zap**, authenticate your KD LLC account using your API key, and follow the setup wizard.

### Make (Integromat)

1. Open your Make workspace and create a new Scenario.
2. Add the **KD LLC** module from the app library.
3. Connect your account by entering your API key when prompted.
4. Choose a trigger (for example, **Watch Reservations**) and chain it to any downstream modules your workflow requires.

<Tip>
If none of the listed integrations meet your needs, use KD LLC's [webhook notifications](/configuration/notifications#webhook-notifications) to build a fully custom integration. Any service that can receive an HTTP POST request — including your own internal APIs — can be connected this way without relying on a third-party automation platform.
</Tip>
