KD LLC is a hospitality and venue management platform designed for the modern dining and nightlife industry. It brings together venue discovery, reservation management, event planning, and guest experience tools into a single, unified platform. Whether you're running a restaurant, managing a nightclub, or orchestrating large-scale private events, KD LLC gives you everything you need to deliver seamless experiences for your guests and your team.

## Key features

KD LLC packs a full suite of hospitality tools into one platform. Explore the core features below.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Venue Discovery" icon="location-dot">
    Search and filter venues by type, capacity, cuisine, and availability. Detailed venue profiles include photos, menus, pricing, and real-time open slots.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reservation Management" icon="calendar-check">
    Accept, modify, and cancel reservations from a centralized dashboard. Set party size limits, manage walk-ins, and automate confirmation messages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Event Planning" icon="star">
    Create ticketed or invite-only events with custom branding. Define schedules, assign staff roles, and coordinate logistics end to end.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guest Management" icon="users">
    Build and segment guest lists, track VIP status, and record preferences. Every guest interaction is logged so your team always has full context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real-Time Availability" icon="clock">
    Expose live availability across all your venues and time slots. Sync with third-party booking channels to eliminate double-bookings automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notifications" icon="bell">
    Send automated confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups via email or SMS. Customize notification templates to match your brand voice.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who KD LLC is for

KD LLC is purpose-built for hospitality professionals who need more than a generic booking tool.

- **Venue operators** use KD LLC to manage floor capacity, coordinate front-of-house staff, and maximize table turnover without sacrificing the guest experience.
- **Event planners** rely on KD LLC to handle everything from initial venue sourcing through post-event reporting, including RSVPs, ticketing, and guest communications.
- **Hospitality businesses** — from independent restaurants to multi-location nightlife groups — use KD LLC's API and integrations to embed booking and event features directly into their own products and websites.
