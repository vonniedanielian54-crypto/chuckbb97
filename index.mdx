## Get up and running in three steps

Follow the steps below to go from zero to your first booked reservation on the KD LLC platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Visit [app.kdllc.com](https://app.kdllc.com) and sign up for a free account. Verify your email address and complete your profile to unlock all platform features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first reservation">
    Browse available venues, select a date and time, and submit a reservation request through the dashboard or via the KD LLC API. You'll receive a confirmation instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore events">
    Discover upcoming dining experiences, nightlife events, and private gatherings. Use the Events section to RSVP, manage guest lists, and track attendance in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
